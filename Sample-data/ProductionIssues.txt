INCIDENT: INC-20250412-07
TITLE: Duplicate debits and negative balances on concurrent transfers
SEVERITY: SEV1
STATUS: Closed
DETECTED: 2025-04-12 08:42 GST (automated balance-exception alert)
RESOLVED: 2025-04-12 15:20 GST
DURATION: 6h 38m
RELATED RULE: BR-003
RELATED DEFECT: DEF-1002
AFFECTED RELEASE: 2.4.3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  During the salary-day peak, 214 accounts were driven into an unauthorised
  negative balance. Customers who tapped Confirm twice, or whose mobile client
  retried on a slow network, produced two simultaneous debits that each passed
  the available-balance check.

IMPACT
  214 accounts affected. Total unauthorised debit exposure AED 1.84 million.
  63 customers contacted the call centre. 9 complaints escalated to the
  regulator's consumer protection channel.

TIMELINE
  08:42  Balance-exception job flags 31 accounts below zero with no overdraft.
  08:55  SEV1 declared. Payments on-call and core banking on-call engaged.
  09:20  Pattern identified: paired transactions within the same 40 ms window.
  09:35  Mobile client confirm button debounced by a hotfix config push.
  11:10  Root cause confirmed - the balance read happened before the database
         transaction opened and no row lock was taken.
  13:45  Fix deployed to production behind a feature flag.
  15:20  All 214 accounts reversed and corrected. Incident closed.

ROOT CAUSE
  TransferValidator read the available balance through a separate read-only
  connection and the posting service did not re-verify inside the transaction.
  Two concurrent requests therefore both observed the pre-debit balance. This
  violates BR-003 acceptance criterion AC3.

RESOLUTION
  Balance re-verification moved inside the posting transaction with a
  SELECT FOR UPDATE lock on the account row. Released in 2.5.0.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-001  Add concurrency regression case TC-FT-032 to the pack.        DONE
  ACT-002  Introduce mandatory idempotency keys (became BR-011).         DONE
  ACT-003  Add a real-time negative-balance alert with a 5-minute SLA.   DONE

INCIDENT: INC-20250708-04
TITLE: Daily limit not released after core banking timeouts
SEVERITY: SEV3
STATUS: Closed
DETECTED: 2025-07-08 12:15 GST (call centre volume spike)
RESOLVED: 2025-07-10 09:00 GST
DURATION: 44h 45m
RELATED RULE: BR-002
RELATED DEFECT: DEF-1016
AFFECTED RELEASE: 2.5.2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  A core banking maintenance window produced a burst of TRF-5001 timeouts.
  The daily limit reservation taken before posting was never released on the
  failure path, so customers whose transfers failed were blocked from retrying
  for the rest of the day.

IMPACT
  1,120 customers carried a phantom limit consumption. 380 calls to the contact
  centre. No financial loss. Reputational impact only.

ROOT CAUSE
  The limits service reserved the amount at validation time and released it
  only in the success and explicit-rejection handlers. The timeout path
  returned early without releasing.

RESOLUTION
  Reservation release moved into a finally block, and a sweeper job now
  releases any reservation older than 10 minutes with no matching posting.
  Released in 2.6.0.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-011  Regression case TC-FT-006 added.                              DONE
  ACT-012  Reservation age dashboard added to the payments Grafana board. DONE

INCIDENT: INC-20250915-02
TITLE: OTP SMS delivery delays during a telecom outage
SEVERITY: SEV2
STATUS: Closed
DETECTED: 2025-09-15 17:05 GST
RESOLVED: 2025-09-15 21:40 GST
DURATION: 4h 35m
RELATED RULE: BR-005
RELATED DEFECT: none - upstream vendor issue
AFFECTED RELEASE: not release related
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  The primary SMS aggregator degraded and OTP delivery times rose from a p90 of
  6 seconds to over 200 seconds. Because the OTP is valid for only 180 seconds
  (BR-005), most codes expired before they arrived and customers received
  TRF-4007 on entry.

IMPACT
  Approximately 8,400 transfers above AED 25,000 could not be completed.
  Transfers below the OTP threshold were unaffected. NFR-006 breached for the
  duration.

ROOT CAUSE
  Vendor-side routing failure at the aggregator. No defect in bank code.

RESOLUTION
  Traffic failed over to the secondary aggregator at 19:50 GST. Delivery
  recovered within 4 minutes of the switch.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-021  Automate aggregator failover on a p90 breach over 30 seconds. DONE
  ACT-022  Proposal to extend OTP validity to 300 seconds.               REJECTED
           Rejected by Information Security - increases the brute force
           window. BR-005 remains at 180 seconds.
  ACT-023  Show a resend button after 30 seconds instead of 60.          DONE

INCIDENT: INC-20260119-05
TITLE: Sanction screening queue backlog holds legitimate transfers
SEVERITY: SEV2
STATUS: Closed
DETECTED: 2026-01-19 10:30 GST
RESOLVED: 2026-01-19 16:15 GST
DURATION: 5h 45m
RELATED RULE: BR-014
RELATED DEFECT: none - capacity issue
AFFECTED RELEASE: 3.0.1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  A screening list refresh loaded a new sanctions file with a broader fuzzy
  matching threshold. Hit volume rose from roughly 40 per day to 2,600, and
  every affected transfer sat in PENDING_COMPLIANCE awaiting manual review by a
  team of four analysts.

IMPACT
  2,600 transfers held, of which 2,571 were later cleared as false positives.
  Average customer wait 4h 10m against an internal target of 30 minutes.

ROOT CAUSE
  The vendor's list update shipped with a default match threshold of 75 percent
  instead of the bank's configured 92 percent. Configuration was not pinned and
  was overwritten by the refresh job.

RESOLUTION
  Threshold restored to 92 percent and pinned in the deployment manifest. The
  backlog was cleared with additional analysts from the AML team.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-031  Pin all screening configuration in version control.           DONE
  ACT-032  Alert when the hit rate deviates more than 3x from baseline.  DONE
  ACT-033  Fail closed on screening timeouts became formal in BR-014.    DONE

INCIDENT: INC-20260403-11
TITLE: Clearing file rejected on Eid holiday
SEVERITY: SEV2
STATUS: Closed
DETECTED: 2026-04-03 07:10 GST
RESOLVED: 2026-04-04 11:30 GST
DURATION: 28h 20m
RELATED RULE: BR-013
RELATED DEFECT: DEF-1012
AFFECTED RELEASE: 3.1.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  Domestic transfers submitted on an Eid public holiday were assigned the
  holiday itself as the value date. The clearing scheme rejected the entire
  outbound file, and 1,860 transfers showed as PROCESSING with no settlement.

IMPACT
  1,860 transfers delayed by one business day. AED 12.4 million in value.
  No funds lost. 240 customer complaints.

ROOT CAUSE
  The holiday calendar table was populated for 2025 only. When the lookup
  returned no row for the 2026 date, the code defaulted to treating the day as
  a business day rather than raising an error. This violates BR-013 AC3.

RESOLUTION
  Holiday calendar loaded for 2026 and 2027. The lookup now raises a hard
  failure on a missing calendar year rather than assuming a business day.
  Full fix targeted for 3.3.0, tracked as DEF-1012.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-041  Annual calendar load added to the December release checklist. DONE
  ACT-042  Regression case TC-FT-040 added.                              DONE
  ACT-043  Alert 60 days before the calendar runs out.                   OPEN

INCIDENT: INC-20260519-02
TITLE: Customers able to exceed the daily transfer limit
SEVERITY: SEV1
STATUS: Closed
DETECTED: 2026-05-19 05:30 GST (fraud monitoring)
RESOLVED: 2026-05-20 02:00 GST
DURATION: 20h 30m
RELATED RULE: BR-002
RELATED DEFECT: DEF-1007
AFFECTED RELEASE: 3.1.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  The daily limit counter reset at 00:00 UTC, which is 04:00 Gulf Standard
  Time. Customers transferring between midnight and 04:00 GST received a fresh
  AED 100,000 allowance at 04:00 on the same calendar day, allowing up to
  AED 200,000 in a single GST day.

IMPACT
  47 customers exceeded the daily limit. Aggregate excess AED 2.1 million.
  Three cases were referred to the fraud team; all were later found to be
  legitimate customer activity. Regulatory limit-control finding raised
  internally.

TIMELINE
  05:30  Fraud monitoring flags 9 customers above AED 100,000 for the day.
  06:15  SEV1 declared.
  07:40  Root cause identified in the limits aggregation query.
  09:00  Temporary mitigation - daily limits reduced to AED 60,000 bank wide.
  22:30  Fix deployed with GST date truncation.
  2026-05-20 02:00  Limits restored to AED 100,000. Incident closed.

ROOT CAUSE
  The aggregation query truncated the transaction timestamp using UTC while
  every other component used GST (UTC+4). The mismatch created a four-hour
  window in which the counter had already reset but the calendar day had not
  changed locally.

RESOLUTION
  All limit calculations converted to Asia/Dubai. Released in 3.2.0. BR-002 was
  amended in BRD v3.2 to state the reset time explicitly.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-051  Regression case TC-FT-031 added for the GST reset boundary.   DONE
  ACT-052  Audit every date truncation in the payments codebase.         DONE
  ACT-053  Add a daily reconciliation of limit consumption vs postings.  DONE

INCIDENT: INC-20260602-08
TITLE: Beneficiary enumeration through inconsistent error responses
SEVERITY: SEV3
STATUS: Open - mitigated
DETECTED: 2026-06-02 14:20 GST (internal penetration test)
RESOLVED: not resolved - mitigation in place
RELATED RULE: BR-004
RELATED DEFECT: DEF-1011
AFFECTED RELEASE: 3.2.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  The penetration test team found that referencing a beneficiary belonging to
  another customer returns HTTP 403 with a distinct body, while a non-existent
  beneficiary returns TRF-4004. The difference allows an authenticated attacker
  to determine which beneficiary IDs exist.

IMPACT
  No customer impact observed. No evidence of exploitation in production logs
  over a 90-day lookback. Rated SEV3 because exploitation requires an
  authenticated session and is heavily rate limited.

ROOT CAUSE
  The ownership check throws AccessDeniedException in a filter that runs before
  the business validation chain, so it never reaches the uniform TRF-4004
  handler.

MITIGATION
  Per-customer rate limit reduced from 60 to 30 requests per minute on the
  beneficiary path. Alerting added for more than 20 failed beneficiary
  references from one session in 5 minutes.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-061  Move the ownership check into the validation chain.           OPEN
  ACT-062  Confirm TC-FT-012 passes after the fix.                       OPEN

INCIDENT: INC-20260721-03
TITLE: Elevated TRF-5002 ledger posting failures after a database failover
SEVERITY: SEV1
STATUS: Closed
DETECTED: 2026-07-21 19:02 GST
RESOLVED: 2026-07-21 20:48 GST
DURATION: 1h 46m
RELATED RULE: NFR-005
RELATED DEFECT: none - infrastructure
AFFECTED RELEASE: 3.2.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  An unplanned failover of the core banking database primary caused connection
  pools in the Fund Transfer Service to hold stale connections. Roughly 34
  percent of transfers returned TRF-5002 for 1 hour 46 minutes.

IMPACT
  Approximately 9,700 failed transfer attempts. No duplicate postings, because
  idempotency keys (BR-011) allowed safe client retries. Availability for July
  fell to 99.91 percent against the 99.95 percent target in NFR-003.

ROOT CAUSE
  The connection pool validation query was configured to run only on borrow
  with a 30-minute idle check, so stale connections survived the failover.

RESOLUTION
  Pool validation switched to test-on-borrow with a 5-second timeout, and the
  failover runbook now includes a rolling restart of the transfer service pods.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-071  Chaos test quarterly database failover in UAT.                OPEN
  ACT-072  Add a synthetic transfer probe every 60 seconds.              DONE

INCIDENT: INC-20260805-01
TITLE: International transfers settled without sanction screening
SEVERITY: SEV1
STATUS: Open
DETECTED: 2026-08-05 11:15 GST (compliance QA during regression cycle)
RESOLVED: not resolved
RELATED RULE: BR-014
RELATED DEFECT: DEF-1017
AFFECTED RELEASE: 3.2.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUMMARY
  Regression case TC-FT-041 revealed that international transfers below
  AED 20,000 never reach the sanction screening service. The screening trigger
  evaluates the amount threshold only and ignores transferType, so BR-014 AC3
  is not enforced.

IMPACT
  Under review. A retrospective batch screening of 90 days of international
  transfers below AED 20,000 covering 61,400 transactions produced 4 matches
  requiring investigation. No confirmed sanctions breach at the time of
  writing. Regulatory notification is being assessed by Compliance.

ROOT CAUSE
  ScreeningTrigger.shouldScreen() checks only amount >= SCREENING_THRESHOLD.
  The international condition was specified in BRD v3.0 but never implemented.

INTERIM CONTROL
  A batch job screens all international transfers at 23:00 GST daily. Any hit
  is reversed the following morning. This is a detective control, not the
  preventive control required by BR-014.

FOLLOW-UP ACTIONS
  ACT-081  Implement the transferType condition. Target release 3.3.0.   OPEN
  ACT-082  Confirm TC-FT-041 passes before release sign-off.             OPEN
  ACT-083  Compliance to decide on regulatory notification by 20-Aug-26. OPEN
